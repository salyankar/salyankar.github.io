Read this first. This tool is not the standard and does not reproduce it.
  • Clause numbers and short titles are referenced so you can find the matching requirement in your own copy of ISO/SAE 21434:2021. Every question and evidence prompt here is written from scratch and is my interpretation, not normative text.
  • The R155 themes are an indicative grouping to help you see type-approval readiness. The regulation's CSMS process requirements sit in §7.2.2 — confirm the specific paragraphs against the current text before relying on this for an approval submission.
  • A real conformance judgement needs an independent assessor and the actual work products. Treat this as a structured conversation starter, not an audit result.
