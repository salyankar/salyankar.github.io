Categories and identifiers follow NIST CSF 2.0 (CSWP 29, February 2024). The 0–4 scale borrows the CSF implementation tier names (Partial, Risk Informed, Repeatable, Adaptive) and applies them per category, a common practice for self-assessment — it is not an official NIST scoring method. Guiding questions summarize each category's outcomes; consult the framework's subcategories for full detail.
