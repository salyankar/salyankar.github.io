Cloud Security Sandbox

Learn cloud security by breaking it.

A practice range for the CCSP domains, built for people who learn by doing. Each mission puts you in a broken cloud environment: a misconfigured network, an exposed database, a risky pull request, a live incident, a contract decision. Change the settings, run the simulation, and see which attacks get through and what breaks for the business.

Open Cloud Security Sandbox ↗ v1 · Oct 2026 · single HTML file
Briefing→Change settings→Run the attacker→Fix what failed→Debrief
  • ◇
    Shared responsibilityFill in who owns each layer across IaaS, PaaS and SaaS, and see what goes unprotected when the line is drawn wrong.
  • ⚿
    Data and keysChoose encryption, key custody, tokenization and masking for a payments dataset without breaking billing or analytics.
  • ◍
    Attack pathsLock down a three-tier app on a live network map: SSRF, exposed databases, lateral movement and ransomware against your backups.
  • ⚑
    Code reviewFlag the hidden flaws in a Terraform and Flask pull request. False positives count against you.
  • ▤
    Incident responseInvestigate a cloud audit log and trace the attacker from a leaked key through persistence to exfiltration.
  • §
    Legal and complianceData transfers, the GDPR 72-hour clock, legal holds, SOC reports, SLA math and crypto-shredding. Every mission ends with a debrief mapped to the exam.

Cyber GRC Sandbox

Risk decisions and audit judgment, practiced.

Governance, risk and compliance built on CRISC and CISA concepts. Draw the lines of responsibility at a bank, untangle the risk vocabulary, score and treat a risk register on a live heat map, classify controls, test a real IT general control against evidence, and run an audit engagement from planning to follow-up.

  • ◎
    Risk languageAppetite vs tolerance, inherent vs residual, KRIs vs KPIs, risk owners, scenarios and ALE.
  • ▦
    Heat mapScore five risks against defined scales and appetite, then choose to accept, mitigate, transfer or avoid each one.

Incident Response Sandbox

An incident is a business event. Run it like one.

Who leads what when ransomware hits: cyber, IT, legal, communications, executives, risk, internal audit, regulators, law enforcement and the insurer. Order the response lifecycle, design a tabletop exercise, then play one: nine timed injects where you decide who to bring in and what to do, with a facilitator debrief after every move.

Data Protection Sandbox

Find the crown jewels, then protect them.

Classify an insurer’s data, learn how a high value asset (HVA) program works, rate a candidate inventory with a scoring rubric, then turn on encryption, access, monitoring, DLP and backup controls for a Tier 1 system. Finish by investigating a departing employee’s data theft across DAM, DLP, CASB and UEBA alerts, and managing data from collection to deletion.

How these are built

Each training follows one exam or discipline, so the missions map to what you would be tested on. The scenarios are designed with Claude, and the companies, keys, logs and sites are fictional, so you can break things without breaking anything real. Where a mission cites a real incident, it’s a public one, summarized for learning.

Your progress is saved in your own browser. Nothing connects to real systems or sends data anywhere. If there's a domain you'd like to see next, get in touch.
