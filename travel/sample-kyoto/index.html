Kyoto, Japan

Sample entry — replace everything below with your own trip. A week of temples, trains, and trying to be somewhere before everyone else was.

The short version

Kyoto rewards early mornings. Every famous place is quiet at seven and impossible at ten, so the whole trip ended up organised around one big thing before breakfast, a slow middle of the day, and one neighbourhood in the evening.

What stood out

  • Fushimi Inari at dawn. Walk past the first two hundred gates — the crowds thin out and the path gets genuinely beautiful.
  • Nishiki Market for breakfast, not lunch. Same stalls, a fraction of the people.
  • The Philosopher's Path is fine. The tiny cafés just off it are the point.

What I'd do differently

Buy the rail pass only if you're actually leaving the city. Carry a proper umbrella. Book the one nice dinner before you land, not after.
